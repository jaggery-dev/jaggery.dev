Privacy

This is about the website and our email. What the Jaggery program sends is on the security page.

This website

No cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels, no fonts or scripts from anywhere else. The site is a few static files, hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub keeps visitors' IP addresses in its logs for security, under GitHub's privacy statement. We can't see those logs or turn them off, and we get no visitor numbers.

Writing to hello@jaggery.dev

When you email us, your message is received by Amazon's email service in Ireland (AWS, eu-west-1), held there for about a day, and forwarded to the person who answers it. We keep your email to reply to you. We don't add you to a mailing list, share your address or sell anything.

To have your messages deleted, write and ask.

Issues and discussions on GitHub

Issues, discussions and pull requests on GitHub are public, and GitHub's privacy statement applies to them. Output you paste from jag doctor shows your machines' names and local addresses: remove anything you'd rather not share before posting. Security problems should be reported privately (see security).

Who we are

Jaggery is an independent open-source project. Write to hello@jaggery.dev with any question about this page.
